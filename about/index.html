Who We Are

Owner-Operated Maritime Excellence

Safe Harbor Maritime is an owner-operated maritime company delivering hands-on, mission-focused support for manned and uncrewed operations.

Our team of experienced maritime professionals, engineers, and advisors operates in the field alongside our clients—ensuring tailored strategies, operational excellence, and mission-ready solutions.

This hands-on approach improves efficiency, builds trust, and drives innovation across every engagement.
